Your visit

What happens when you come in

  1. Request a time

    Send a request online or phone the practice.

  2. We confirm

    The practice contacts you with an available time.

  3. See the doctor

    Talk through your concern in a private consulting room.

  4. Leave with a plan

    Treatment, results, forms or a referral, explained clearly.

Our promise

Private, respectful care for sensitive health needs

Some visits are hard to talk about. Family planning, pregnancy and HIV tests, Pap smears and circumcision are handled with discretion, and you can always ask questions.

  • Confidential. Your health information and results stay between you and the doctor.
  • No judgement. Every patient is treated with the same respect.
  • Clear explanations. You will know what is happening and why.
  • Referral when needed. If you need a specialist, the doctor will tell you and help you take the next step.
Questions

Frequently asked

Can’t find your answer? Contact the practice.

How do I make an appointment?

Use the appointment request form on the Contact page, or phone the practice. The practice will contact you to confirm a date and time. Your appointment is only booked once the practice confirms it.

Do you see walk-in patients?

Please contact the practice to find out whether walk-ins are seen and at what times.

Is my visit confidential?

Yes. Your consultation, test results and medical information are kept confidential. Tests such as HIV and pregnancy tests are done privately, and results are given to you directly.

Do you accept medical aid?

Please contact the practice for information about medical aid and consultation fees.

What should I bring for a PDP medical?

Bring your ID, your driving licence, any forms from the licensing office, and your glasses or contact lenses if you wear them.

Do I need to say why I am coming?

No. On the appointment form you can choose “I would rather discuss it with the doctor”. You never need to share personal medical details online.

Need to see the doctor?

Send an appointment request and the practice will contact you to confirm a time.
