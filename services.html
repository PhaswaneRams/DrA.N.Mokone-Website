Consultation

See the doctor about an illness, injury, symptom or general health concern.

A consultation is time with the doctor to talk about what is bothering you, be examined, and agree on a plan. That may include treatment, a prescription, tests, a sick note, or a referral where needed.

Consultations are for adults and children. Bring any medicines you are currently taking so the doctor has the full picture.

What to expect

  • A conversation about your symptoms and history
  • A physical examination where needed
  • A clear explanation and next steps

What to bring

  • Your ID or clinic card
  • Medicines you currently take
  • Medical aid card, if you have one

Family planning

Private, judgement-free advice on contraception and planning a family.

Family planning helps you choose when, and whether, to have children. The doctor will talk through contraception options that suit your health and your plans, and answer questions.

Please ask the practice which contraceptive methods are currently available.

What to expect

  • A private discussion of your options
  • A check of anything that affects which method is suitable
  • Advice on follow-up visits

What to bring

  • Details of any contraception you use now
  • Questions you would like to ask
Request this service

Wound dressing

Cleaning, dressing and checking cuts, grazes and healing wounds.

Proper cleaning and dressing helps a wound heal and lowers the risk of infection. The doctor can also check a wound that is not healing as expected.

For deep wounds, heavy bleeding or serious injuries, go to the nearest emergency unit straight away.

What to expect

  • Cleaning and assessment of the wound
  • A fresh dressing
  • Advice on caring for it at home and when to return

What to bring

  • Any discharge notes if the wound was treated elsewhere
Request this service

Pregnancy test

A confidential test, with time to talk about the result and what comes next.

A pregnancy test checks for the pregnancy hormone. Whatever the result, you can talk it through with the doctor in private and get advice on the next steps.

What to expect

  • A confidential test
  • Your result explained
  • Advice on next steps, including antenatal care or other options

What to bring

  • The date of your last period, if you know it
Request this service

Medical male circumcision

Circumcision performed by a doctor, with a consultation beforehand and aftercare advice.

Medical male circumcision is the surgical removal of the foreskin, done by a doctor in a clinical setting with local anaesthetic.

You will have a consultation first so the doctor can explain the procedure, check that it is suitable, and answer questions. Please ask the practice about age requirements, consent and preparation.

What to expect

  • A consultation before the procedure
  • The procedure under local anaesthetic
  • Clear aftercare instructions and a follow-up check

What to bring

  • Your ID
  • A parent or guardian, for a minor

Pap smear

Cervical screening that checks for early changes in the cells of the cervix.

A Pap smear is a quick screening test. A small sample of cells is taken from the cervix and checked for changes, so that anything needing attention can be found early.

It is done in private, and you can ask for the doctor to explain each step as it happens. The doctor will tell you how often you should be screened.

What to expect

  • A short, private procedure
  • Advice on when and how you will get the result
  • Guidance on how often to be screened

What to bring

  • The date of your last period, if you know it
  • Details of any previous Pap smear results

PDP medical forms

Medical examination and completion of the medical certificate for a Professional Driving Permit.

To apply for or renew a Professional Driving Permit (PDP), you need a medical certificate completed by a doctor. The doctor examines you and completes the form.

Check with the licensing office which forms they need, and bring them with you.

What to expect

  • A medical examination
  • A vision check
  • Completion of the PDP medical certificate

What to bring

  • Your ID
  • Your driving licence
  • The forms from the licensing office
  • Your glasses or contact lenses, if you wear them
Request this service

Eye test

A vision check, for example for driving or PDP applications.

An eye test checks how clearly you see. It is part of PDP and driving-related medical checks, and is useful if you have noticed changes in your vision.

If the doctor finds a problem, you may be referred to an optometrist or eye specialist.

What to expect

  • A reading-chart vision check
  • Advice on whether you need a referral

What to bring

  • Your glasses or contact lenses, if you wear them
Request this service

HIV test

Confidential HIV testing with counselling before and after the test.

Knowing your HIV status helps you protect your health and the people you care about. Testing is confidential, and you will have counselling before and after the test.

If you test positive, the doctor will talk you through what it means and the next steps for treatment and care.

What to expect

  • Counselling before the test
  • A confidential test
  • Your result explained, with support and next steps

What to bring

  • Nothing special — just yourself
