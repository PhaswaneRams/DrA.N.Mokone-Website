Privacy policy

How Dr A.N. Mokone’s practice handles information shared through this website. Template, to be reviewed before publication.

Information we receive

This website does not store anything you type into the appointment form. When you send a request by WhatsApp, email or phone, the practice receives your name, contact details, preferred date and time, and the reason you choose.

How we use it

Only to respond to your request and arrange your appointment. We do not sell your information.

Medical information

Please do not send medical details through the website or messaging apps. Information about your health is collected during your consultation and kept confidential as part of your medical record. To confirm: how records are stored and for how long

Your rights

Under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) you may ask to see, correct or delete your personal information, and you may complain to the Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za).
